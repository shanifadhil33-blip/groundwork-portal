# Pending decisions in the sandbox

New sandboxes and resets contain a Ravi leave request, his sample filter question, and a company lesson proposed by Sam about keeping job notes short. These are fictional sample decisions inserted during setup, without external delivery. They are not evidence of AI learning. The leave date is the first future working day without booked jobs or time off in the workspace calendar.

Sam can approve or decline leave on the dashboard and approve or correct the question. Ravi sees his leave status and answer on Today. Imran can accept or reject the lesson on the dashboard. Only acceptance adds the company rule under Settings, Agent. Reset creates fresh pending examples for trying the other choices. Personal memory starts empty. The ordinary permissions and refusal to approve leave over booked jobs still apply.

## Repeatable habit sequence

1. In a fresh sandbox, use Sam to create three plumbing jobs at Marina Exchange and assign them to Ravi. Leave their visit times unset so no booked visit or day off conflicts.
2. Switch to Ravi and finish each job with a report through the ordinary job controls.
3. Open Today as Ravi. The habit panel offers Remember this area for Dubai Marina. This suggestion comes from at least three recorded completed jobs in the same area, not from a model inferring a preference.
4. Press Remember to save the area. Before that action no area preference is written. Afterward the same area is no longer offered for remembering.
5. Reset to clear these interactions and restore the pending decisions.

Local service tests cover decision outcomes, permission boundaries, isolation, reset, booked leave refusal, static translations and the habit threshold. Browser and live verification are left to the reviewer.
